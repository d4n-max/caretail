1. Home dashboard
2. Pet profile
3. Reminders list

TikTok caption:
Pet care details get scattered fast. CareTail keeps profiles, reminders, diary notes, documents, and care history together in one calm Android app. Not medical advice, just a better place to stay organized.

Hashtags:
#PetCare #PetParents #DogTok #CatTok #PetRoutine #PetReminders #PetRecords #AndroidApps #CareTail

Suggested background music vibe:
Soft upbeat lo-fi pop or cozy productivity beat. Aim for warm, clean, "Sunday reset" energy: gentle percussion, light synth or acoustic plucks, no dramatic drops. Keep volume low enough that text/screenshots carry the story.

Production notes:
- Export each final slide as 1080 x 1920 PNG.
- Keep hooks to one or two short lines.
- Use real screenshot UI only; do not recreate or redesign app screens.
- Preserve readable app text, especially the visible feature labels.
- Use simple motion if turning into video: slow push-in, 2.2-2.8 seconds per slide, quick swipe transitions.
