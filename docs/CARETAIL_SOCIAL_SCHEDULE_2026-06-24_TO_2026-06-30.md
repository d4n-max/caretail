## Quick Production Rules

- Real CareTail UI should be visible in every product post.
- Screenshots, emulator recordings, and device recordings are all okay.
- AI-generated mockup frames or backgrounds are okay if they do not alter or misrepresent the app.
- Keep text readable on mobile.
- Use calm CTAs.
- Avoid diagnosis, treatment, prevention, emergency-care, or vet-replacement claims.
- Do not claim cloud sync, iPhone support, family sharing, or AI features unless they are live.
