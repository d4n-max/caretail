# Privacy Policy Notes For v1.1

CareTail v1.1 adds optional Google Sign-In through Firebase Authentication.

Policy notes to include in the public Privacy Policy:

- CareTail supports optional Google Sign-In.
- CareTail can still be used without an account.
- If a user signs in, Firebase Authentication may process Google account identifiers such as email address, display name, profile photo, and unique Firebase user ID.
- Google Sign-In is used to prepare for future backup and sync support.
- CareTail does not upload pet care records to cloud services in this version.
- Pet profiles, reminders, health diary entries, document records, and generated care reports remain stored locally on the device in this version.
- Users can sign out from Settings.
- Users can delete their CareTail account sign-in from Settings.
- Users can choose whether account deletion also deletes local app data on the device.
- Users can request account deletion through the public account deletion/privacy page.
- Premium remains handled by Google Play Billing.
- CareTail does not add Firestore pet records, Firebase Storage, FCM, or Firebase Analytics for this release.
